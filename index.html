What you're reading

Leadership Legacy is the capstone program of Fogarty Ripple, for UWA Scholars ready to direct their energy toward something bigger than themselves. Over five weeks they went deep on a challenge facing Western Australia, dug beneath the symptoms to find its root cause, and tested their approach to change-making with stakeholders working in the sector. The WA2050 Manifesto is the final product of their thinking.
